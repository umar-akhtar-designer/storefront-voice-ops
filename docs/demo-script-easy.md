# EASY video script — just read the lines 🎬

Total video: 5 minutes. You say the quote lines ("...") out loud.

---

## SETUP (do before recording)

1. Terminal: `npm run dev` → LEAVE IT RUNNING. Do nothing in that window.
2. Browser: open `http://localhost:3000`
3. Allow microphone.
4. Top right → pick **Kapraa.pk**.
5. Press `Cmd + Shift + 5` → click "Record Selected Portion" → select the whole browser → press Record.
6. Now start reading below, in order.

---

## SCENE 1 — the problem (1 minute)

WAIT 2 seconds. Then read:

> "Small sellers in Pakistan sell on WhatsApp and Instagram.
> They have no website. Orders are written in chats.
> They forget orders. They ship late. Customers complain.
>
> Sellers who have a website — Shopify, or a PHP website — still do everything by clicking.
> Click, open, click, ship. Every day. Many times a day.
>
> I asked: what if the seller can just TALK, and the store does the work?"

DO: show the browser for a moment. No clicks yet.

---

## SCENE 2 — this is my project (20 seconds)

> "This is Storefront Voice Ops. A voice helper for any store.
> You talk. The store works. It always asks before touching real orders.
> It writes every action to a log, so you can see what it did."

DO: point mouse at the chat window ("Talk to your store").

---

## SCENE 3 — live demo on a REAL store (2 minutes) ⭐ most important

This is kapraa.pk — my real website. It is connected live.

**Q1 — sales. TYPE in the chat box:**
`How did my store do today?`
Wait for reply. Read it silently, then say:

> "This is real data from my kapraa.pk website right now."

**Q2 — stock. TYPE:**
`What is running low?`
Wait. Then say:

> "These are real products from my store with stock getting low."

**Q3 — orders. SAY (with mic):**
"What orders are pending?"
Wait. Then say:

> "Real orders. Real customers. Live from my website."

**Q4 — ship an order. SAY:**
"Ship order [say one ID from the list] with Leopards."
A Yes/No card appears. Click **Yes**.

> "See — it asked me first. Only my yes makes it real. And here (scroll down) the
> audit log shows the order was shipped."

**Q5 — inspector. SCROLL to "Inspector" panel.**

> "Everything it did is written here. Tools it used. Time. Result. Full proof."

---

## SCENE 4 — the best part (1.5 minutes) ⭐ say it proud

Click **+ Add store** (top right). Show the dropdown. Point at each line while saying:

> "Here is what makes this project different.
>
> One: it works with **Shopify** stores.
> Two: it works with **WooCommerce** stores.
> Three: it works with **any custom-coded website** — even a simple PHP site. I connected
> kapraa.pk — my own PHP website — with one small helper file.
>
> And four — the biggest one: what if you have NO website?
> You sell on WhatsApp? On Instagram, in DMs? No problem.
> Just click Manual, add your products here, record your orders here —
> and this voice helper does the same work for you. Shipping, stock, everything.
>
> And if you have many stores? Add one profile here… switch any time here.
> One app. All your stores."

Close the popup with **Cancel**.

---

## SCENE 5 — safe by design (30 seconds)

> "Voice can break a real store. So this app is careful.
> Shipping and cancel always ask Yes/No first.
> Old requests expire. Everything is rate-limited and logged.
> And there is a Practice store — fake data — so anyone can try it with zero risk."

---

## SCENE 6 — finish (15 seconds)

> "From a WhatsApp group to a Shopify giant — one voice helper for every kind of store.
> Full code is open source on GitHub. Thank you."

WAIT 3 seconds on the app screen. Then stop recording.

---

## If something goes wrong while recording

- Mic hears you wrong? → TYPE the same words instead. Say: "same thing works by typing too."
- App slow or error toast? → just keep going and SKIP that line. Judges never see the best take, only the final.
- Do not restart recording from zero. Finish scene by scene.
